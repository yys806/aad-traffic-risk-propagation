# Sun & Tan (2020) — On a class of new nonlocal traffic flow models with look-ahead rules

- **Venue / DOI**: *Physica D: Nonlinear Phenomena*; `10.1016/j.physd.2020.132663`.
- **Zotero**: item `VIPIJ222`, attachment `4SH24ZBD`.
- **Reading**: `FULL_TEXT_READ` (full indexed PDF).

## Question and method

The paper constructs one-dimensional cellular-automata models with nonlocal look-ahead rules. Two rules use distance to the front vehicle or density ahead, with stochastic Arrhenius-type transitions and a multiple-move mechanism. A semi-discrete process is coarse-grained to macroscopic dynamics and simulated with a list-based kinetic Monte Carlo algorithm.

## Evidence and results

KMC fluxes agree with coarse-grained averaged fluxes under several parameter settings. The setting is a single-lane periodic loop and focuses on fundamental-diagram behavior.

## What is actually shown

Look-ahead interaction can be represented mathematically and can change aggregate flux. There is no V2X message lifecycle, causal warning treatment, or network risk outcome.

## Limitations and AAD relation

The closed loop, homogeneous geometry and stochastic rule limit direct transfer to AAD. It is useful background for distinguishing physical look-ahead from information-mediated exposure; it does not support a novel AAD claim by itself.
