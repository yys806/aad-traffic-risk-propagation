## Verified contribution

The abstract describes a dynamic ad hoc network in which information moves through both vehicle motion and short-range broadcast hops. The work studies penetration rate, information propagation efficiency and a traffic-jam example using analysis and microscopic simulation, including opposite-direction relay vehicles.
