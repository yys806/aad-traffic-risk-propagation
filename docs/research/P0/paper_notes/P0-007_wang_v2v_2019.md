# Wang et al. (2019) — Multiclass Information Flow Propagation Control under V2V Communication Environments

- **Identifier**: arXiv:1901.11133; Jian Wang, Srinivas Peeta, Lili Lu, Tao Li.
- **Source**: official arXiv record and abstract; no Zotero local attachment.
- **Reading**: `PARTIAL_READ`.

## Verified contribution

The paper proposes a queue-based two-layer model: an integro-differential upper layer for V2V information dissemination and an LWR lower layer for traffic flow. It derives asymptotic informed-vehicle density, information-flow propagation-wave conditions and speed, with numerical homogeneous/heterogeneous experiments.

## AAD relation

It directly covers information-wave propagation constrained by traffic dynamics. It does not establish controller adoption, actual braking/acceleration, TTC/P2 outcomes or a network-level causal risk effect. AAD must add those links rather than relabel the information wave.
