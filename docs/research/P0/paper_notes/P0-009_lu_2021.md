## AAD relation

The paper evaluates surrogate safety measures against naturalistic driving data. It is retained as a measurement-validity threat: a change in TTC/DRAC/PET cannot be treated as a proven change in crash risk without an applicability argument. Full method and numeric results remain unavailable in this audit.
