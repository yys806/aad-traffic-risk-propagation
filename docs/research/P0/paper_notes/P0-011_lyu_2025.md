# Lyu et al. (2025) — A real-time conflict risk prediction modeling based on causal inference and graph-model

- **Venue / DOI**: *Transportation Research Part C* 178:105219; `10.1016/j.trc.2025.105219`.
- **Zotero**: item `VGEWHVL9`, attachment `4G6QEQJN`.

## Question and method

The paper proposes a full-chain prediction framework for multi-configuration expressway diversion zones. It extracts two-dimensional conflict incidents and traffic variables, uses a structural causal model (SAM) to discover causal relations, reconstructs causal-structure data, and feeds the result to graph attention networks. The TSC risk indicator and TTC-related conflict definitions are used in the risk labeling pipeline.

## Evidence and results

Drone-collected trajectories are used. Tables 7–8 compare GAT/GCN configurations and risk classes; the high-risk F1 values are high in the reported configurations. The model improves prediction and interpretable variable relations but is still a prediction study, not a randomized information intervention.

## What is actually shown

The authors show predictive utility and a model-based causal graph under their assumptions. They do not show that a remote message is delivered, adopted, causes a physical action, or reduces regional risk.

## AAD relation

It is a strong neighboring method for causal risk modeling. AAD must distinguish causal discovery for prediction from causal identification of an intervention under network interference.
