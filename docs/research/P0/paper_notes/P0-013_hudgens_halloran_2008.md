## Verified contribution

The paper formalizes causal effects when one unit’s outcome can depend on other units’ treatment, using group-level/individual-level randomization under partial interference. It separates direct and indirect effects and emphasizes that estimands depend on assignment strategies.

## AAD relation

AAD vehicles are interacting units. Any regional risk effect requires an explicit interference structure, exposure mapping and estimand; ordinary SUTVA-based A/B contrasts are insufficient. Exact variance and application details require full reading before implementation.
