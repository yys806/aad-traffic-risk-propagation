## Verified contribution

The paper gives randomization-based estimators for average causal effects when treatment assignments interfere through a known exposure mapping. It combines an assignment design, a mapping from assignments to exposures and estimands, with inverse-probability weighting and variance estimators under local dependence.

## AAD relation

The exposure-mapping idea is the statistical template for separating message exposure, physical neighbor exposure and total regional effect. AAD cannot claim a new interference framework without a transportation-specific mechanism and evidence that the mapping is measured rather than assumed.
