# Coifman & Li (2024) — Partial trajectory method to align and validate successive video cameras for vehicle tracking

- **Venue / DOI**: *Transportation Research Part C* 158:104416; `10.1016/j.trc.2023.104416`.
- **Zotero**: item `SZL54NKN`, attachment `FDMRDKC3`.
- **Reading**: `FULL_TEXT_READ` (full 19-page PDF).

## Question and method

The paper develops a partial-trajectory method to estimate spatial offsets and scale factors between successive camera views. It uses polynomial fits to matched partial trajectories and an iterative simulated-gap procedure; Algorithm 3 selects the scale factor that minimizes offset variation.

## Evidence and results

The authors show systematic gradients in the original NGSIM camera alignment and estimate scale factors ranging roughly 0.97–1.05. After correction, relative errors are below 1% in the reported camera pairs (Fig. 10–11, pp. 19 onward).
