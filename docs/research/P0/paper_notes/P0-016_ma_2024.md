# Ma et al. (2024) — Progressive virtual risk-based vehicle trajectory optimization in mixed traffic flow

- **Venue / DOI**: *Transportation Research Part C* 165:104701; `10.1016/j.trc.2024.104701`.
- **Zotero**: item `7XL7PTAX`, attachment `SI87KLBX`.
- **Reading**: `FULL_TEXT_READ` (full indexed PDF).

## Question and method

The paper proposes virtual risk-based trajectory optimization for mixed traffic and ramp merging. It uses a risk-quantification model (RQM), incremental vehicle projection and distributed longitudinal planning to create merging gaps and reduce oscillation while retaining efficiency.

## Evidence and results

Four simulation experiments cover car-following and pre-merging conditions. The paper reports lower oscillation amplitude and safe/efficient trajectories under its model assumptions.

## AAD relation

Risk-based control and virtual risk are established engineering mechanisms. They do not imply remote message arrival, human/controller adoption or a causal regional risk effect; this work is a useful control baseline and a warning against naming a risk surrogate as a new propagation phenomenon.
