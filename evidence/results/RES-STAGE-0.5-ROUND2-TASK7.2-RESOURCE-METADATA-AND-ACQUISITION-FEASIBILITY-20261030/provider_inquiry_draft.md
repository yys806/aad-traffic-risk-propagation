1. Which license applies specifically to these raw AVIs? The current video record states CC BY-SA 4.0, the video metadata attachment mentions CC BY 2.0, and the separate trajectory/support record states BY-SA 3.0 and retains historical RDE restrictions. Please clarify the applicable attribution, share-alike and redistribution conditions, including research analysis, journal presentation, derived still images/crops/annotations and aggregate result publication. 3. What are the actual capture start/end times and timezone for each file? Are the names nominal intervals? The trajectory supports extend beyond the nominal boundaries. Camera 7's first-window file is much smaller than the others; is it complete, or encoded/duration-limited differently?
4. Are absolute timestamps, PTS/time-base information, nominal/measured FPS, fixed/variable-rate information, or frame-loss/repetition logs available? Is there a validated mapping from trajectory Frame_ID/Global_Time to raw-video frames?
5. Are per-camera synchronization calibration records, offsets or drift estimates available, particularly for cameras 7/8?
6. Is there documentation of period-specific Local/Global coordinate origins or transformations? Our limited coordinate correspondence fits differ mainly by approximately 12 m; we have not interpreted this as physical displacement or changed the formal data. We would also welcome the authoritative camera-to-Local_Y map.
